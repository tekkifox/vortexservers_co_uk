# Cookie Policy

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

This Cookie Policy explains how VorteX Servers ("we", "us", "our") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website at vortexservers.co.uk (the "Site"). It should be read alongside our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) and [Terms of Service](/terms).

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to recognise your device, remember preferences, and collect information about how you use the site. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, and pixels.

## How we use cookies

We use the following categories of cookies:

### Essential cookies

These are necessary for the Site to function and cannot be switched off. They are usually set in response to actions you take, such as logging in or filling in forms. Without them, parts of the Site would not work.

- **Session cookies** — maintain your login session while you browse.
- **Security cookies** — help protect against cross-site request forgery and other attacks.
- **Preference cookies** — remember basic settings such as theme or layout choices.

### Analytics cookies

Where enabled, these help us understand how visitors use the Site so we can improve it. They collect information such as pages visited, time spent on the site, and navigation paths. All data is aggregated and anonymised where possible.

### Third-party cookies

Some features of the Site are provided by third parties, who may set their own cookies. These include:

- **Embedded media** (e.g. Discord widgets, YouTube videos) — governed by the third party's own cookie policy.
- **Payment providers** — if you complete a purchase, the payment processor may set cookies to process the transaction securely.

We do not control third-party cookies. Please refer to the relevant third party's privacy and cookie policies.

## Managing cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

- View cookies stored on your device
- Delete all or specific cookies
- Block cookies from particular sites
- Block all third-party cookies
- Clear cookies when you close the browser

Please note that blocking or deleting essential cookies may affect the functionality of the Site, and some features may not work as intended.

## Cookie consent

Where required by law, we will ask for your consent before setting non-essential cookies. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing cookies in your browser or contacting us.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

## Contact
