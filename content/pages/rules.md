Breaking of rules depending on the severity can result in bans. So be warned.

1. No disrespecting or insulting other people in the server.
2. No purposeful spamming of any kind.
3. No NSFW Content: In the form of SENDING said content. Swearing and vulgar language is okay as long as it isn't making someone feel uncomfortable, staff will judge these situations on a case by case basis.
4. No Hateful Racism.
5. No Hateful Homophobia.
6. No advertising other servers / direct messaging other servers to our players.
