Feel free to invite your friends here to come play games and hang out with us all, we aim to make this a pleasant and fun community to be a part of, currently we host a variety of different servers for various games including:

* Minecraft
* Unturned
* Terraria
* V Rising
* 7 Days to Die
* Factorio
* Palworld
* Project Zomboid
* Rust

In the future we may even broaden our horizons and add more to that list! Feel free to give your suggestions in the [support channel](https://discord.com/channels/581208549321998346/1499609117889663168) in the discord server. Each game has their own voice channels and various themed text channels to talk to other people in the community!
